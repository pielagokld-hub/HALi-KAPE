HOW IT ALL BEGAN

Our Story

HALi' KAPE began in 2023 as a humble night-market beverage stall at Mercato Lucena. With affordable iced coffees and refreshing drinks, it gradually gained the support of customers who enjoyed its products and friendly atmosphere.

In 2024, the business took another step forward by opening a permanent, air-conditioned cafe on Hermana Fausta Street in Lucena City. With a rooftop patio and free Wi-Fi, it became a place where students, professionals, and friends could relax and spend time together.

By 2026, HALi' KAPE expanded its menu to include silog meals, sizzling plates, and trending desserts. Through its main branch and mobile campus pop-ups, the business continued to bring its food and beverages closer to the community.

From our humble beginnings to our growing menu, our journey continues with a simple purpose: to make every visit worth remembering.
